HOW IT WORKS NOW:
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DRIVER FLOW:
  1. New driver registers with phone + OTP
  2. Navigate to "Upload Documents" screen
  3. See all 9 document cards
  4. Upload documents 1-8: Choose Camera or Gallery
  5. Upload document 9 (DRIVER_SELFIE): Click → Camera auto-launches
  6. Once all 9 uploaded: "Submit for Verification" button enables
  7. Submit all 9 documents
  8. Status changes to 'pending_review'
  9. See "Waiting for Approval" screen
  10. Super admin reviews and approves all 9
  11. Driver can now login to dashboard

ADMIN FLOW:
  1. Super admin logs into dashboard
  2. Goes to "Verification Dashboard"
  3. Sees drivers with 'pending_review' status
  4. Clicks driver to view all 9 documents
  5. Reviews each of the 9 documents
  6. Can approve or reject each document
  7. Once all 9 approved: Driver status changes to 'approved'
  8. Driver gets full access
