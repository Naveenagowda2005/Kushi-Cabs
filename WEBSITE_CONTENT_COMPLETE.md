- Drivers can cancel trips up to 2 minutes after acceptance
- After 2 minutes: 50% cancellation charge
- After 10 minutes: Full fare charged as cancellation fee
- Multiple cancellations may result in account suspension

#### Cancellation Procedure:
1. Open active trip in app
2. Tap "Cancel Trip" button
3. Select reason from dropdown
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Cancellation fee charged immediately (if applicable)

#### Valid Reasons for Cancellation:
- Vehicle breakdown or emergency
- Unexpected traffic or road closure
- Medical emergency
- System error or miscommunication
- Unsafe conditions or passenger behavior
- Personal emergency (immediate attention required)

#### Penalty System:
- 1-2 cancellations per week: Warning
- 3-5 cancellations per week: 10% commission deduction
- 6+ cancellations per week: 20% commission deduction
- Repeated violations: Temporary account suspension (3-7 days)
- Excessive violations: Permanent account termination

### 2. Vendor Cancellation

#### Policy:
- Vendors can cancel trips up to 5 minutes after posting
- Between 5-15 minutes: 25% trip value charged
- After 15 minutes: 50% trip value charged
- After 30 minutes (driver at pickup): 100% trip value charged

#### Cancellation Procedure:
1. Open active trip
2. Select "Cancel Trip"
3. Provide cancellation reason
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Driver notified automatically

#### Valid Reasons:
- Trip information error or misunderstanding
- Unexpected schedule change
- Safety concerns
- Vehicle maintenance required
- Medical emergency

#### Penalty Structure:
- Up to 2 cancellations per week: No penalty
- 3-5 cancellations per week: Warning and 5% earnings reduction
- 6+ cancellations per week: 10% earnings reduction
- Repeated violations: Account suspension review

### 3. Cancellation Charges Breakdown

**Driver Initiates Cancellation:**
- First 2 minutes: Free

**Vendor Initiates Cancellation:**
- First 5 minutes: Free
