### 7. FAQ SECTION

**Q: Is Kushi Cabs available in my city?**
A: Check our service area map on the app. We're expanding to new cities regularly.

**Q: How do I sign up?**
A: Download the app, enter your phone number, verify with OTP, and complete identity verification.

**Q: What documents do I need?**
A: Drivers: License, Aadhar, Vehicle registration. Vendors: Aadhar, PAN, Bank details.

**Q: How are fares calculated?**
A: Distance × Base Rate + Time × Rate/Minute + Surge Pricing (if applicable)

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, anytime. Visit Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent.

**Q: How do I report unsafe behavior?**
A: Tap "Report" after trip or contact support@kushicabs.com immediately.

**Q: What is the commission rate?**
A: Vendor commission is set by admin and visible in your dashboard. Typically 10-20%.

**Q: How do I withdraw earnings?**
A: Wallet → Withdraw → Select amount → Bank transfer processed in 1-2 days.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: All trips use Razorpay (Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, Wallets)

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. We use end-to-end encryption and comply with data protection laws.
```
