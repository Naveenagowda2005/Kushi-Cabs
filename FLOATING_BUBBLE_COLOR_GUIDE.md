## 🎨 Color Harmony

### Color Scheme Type: **Modern Professional**

- **Primary**: Blue (#0066CC) - Trust, professionalism, GPS/location
- **Accent**: Red (#FF6B6B) - Alert, important notifications
- **Neutral**: White (#FFFFFF) - Clean background
- **Secondary**: Gray (#333333, #EEEEEE) - Text, dividers

### Why These Colors?

1. **Blue (#0066CC)**
   - ✅ Represents GPS/location (maps are blue)
   - ✅ Professional appearance
   - ✅ Stands out without being aggressive
   - ✅ Good for ride-sharing apps (trust color)

2. **Red (#FF6B6B)**
   - ✅ Alert color (trip count = urgent info)
   - ✅ Contrasts well with blue
   - ✅ Not too aggressive (softer red)

3. **White (#FFFFFF)**
   - ✅ Clean, modern look
   - ✅ High contrast for readability
   - ✅ Professional appearance

4. **Gray (#333333, #EEEEEE)**
   - ✅ Neutral, readable
   - ✅ Subtle dividers
   - ✅ Doesn't distract from primary content
